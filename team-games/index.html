Deck of Us

Your team as a trading card game.

Every person becomes a card: custom chibi art, five stats drawn from their real work, two signature powers, and a fun fact. Printed on professional trading card stock, the same 2.5 by 3.5 inches as the cards people already collect.

The live event brings it together. Players pair up and combine their cards against scenarios projected on screen. When a scenario matches a power on the table, bonus points fire. To win, you have to know what your teammates are good at. That is the point.

Case study

The Analytics Card Battle

A 20-person corporate analytics team was working through the SERVE leadership model, and one principle kept surfacing: engage and develop others. Which raises an honest question. How do you develop people you barely know?

Eight weeks later, every person on the team was a trading card. A short survey became five stats per person. Each card got two signature powers, a fun fact, and custom art. Twenty people, twenty cards, one deck that could only belong to this team.

Within minutes of the first round, people were trading. Everyone wanted their own card, and some campaigned hard for specific teammates.

"I just need to trade for a Debbie!"

The next day, people were still trading, trying to assemble their full teams. One player quietly swapped his ID badge photo for his player card.

"How are you going to top this one?"

"That was so fun. Can you make cards for my family?"

"I had no idea what to expect, but it definitely wasn't this," said with a grin.
